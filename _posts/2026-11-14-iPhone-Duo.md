On the morning of October 16th, I woke up at 5am and prepared to try and order the new iPhone Duo. I had done all of the pre-ordering steps, so it was a matter of tapping fast inside the Apple Store app on my iPhone. I was one of the lucky ones. I received my iPhone Duo on Monday, October 26th, only three days past the launch day. I felt very fortunate given how many weren’t as lucky and had ship dates into January. In 2007 I had the original iPhone, and have upgraded consistently for the past, almost, 20 years. I never considered wanting a foldable phone and didn’t see their appeal, but the iPhone Duo caught my attention. I haven’t been this excited for a new iPhone model in a long time. I may still be in the honeymoon phase, but I am having so much fun with the Duo. 

<!--more-->

## Hardware: Size, Weight, and Display

### Folded

One of my favorite iPhones was the iPhone 12 mini. I loved how pocketable the mini was and how light and thin it felt in my hand. The iPhone Duo isn’t light, but when folded, the shorter vertical space and how it feels in my pocket remind me of my iPhone 12 mini. I don’t mind the smaller outer display. I like how compact it is and find the vertical dock and toolbar to be a creative solution. I also find typing on the outer display to be easier and more accurate than my previous iPhone 14 Pro, due to the wider horizontal space. The weight is noticeable when folded, but it doesn’t bother me in my pocket, and feels substantial in my hand. 

### Unfolded

I have never had an iPad mini, but I am an avid iPad Pro user. The hardware, hinge, and inner display are remarkable on the iPhone Duo. Apple nailed the industrial design and polish with how it feels to unfold the device. The nano texture display completely hides the crease and is beautiful to look at in various lighting conditions. I love being able to unfold the device for reading, looking at PDFs, or browsing photos and watching video. The nano texture not only looks great, but it feels great. Touch and Apple Pencil both feel natural on that nano texture surface. If I hold the nano texture at an angle and into the light, I can see that there are a lot of fingerprints, but when using the display and looking at it straight on, I don’t notice any fingerprints. Regarding weight, when unfolded the device feels remarkably thin and light. 

## Camera

I am still getting used to the camera system. I have never had a camera control button and there is definitely a learning curve with the touch sensitive camera control. Taking pictures is most natural when the device is closed. When unfolded, it is awkward to hold and odd that when I am holding the device in portrait orientation, the photo is taken in landscape, and vice versa. The picture quality is comparable to my iPhone 14 Pro, minus the zoom lens. 

## Ergonomics

When folded, the device is easy to hold, but a bit heavy. When unfolded it is not as easy to hold. I am getting more used to it. I like that the bezels are incredibly thin and the screen goes to the edge, but that does make holding it a challenge. Sometimes I inadvertantly put the apps into wiggle mode because I have rested a finger on the home screen. I added a [MagSafe Ring]([](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H2BS2DHP?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title)), which gives me something to hold onto and also doubles as a stand. I actually really like this inexpensive accessory and it makes holding the device much easier. I need to try out tent and book mode more. Primarily I only use the device in fully open or closed modes. 

Pinning two apps side by side when unfolded is a creative way to be productive. I have pinned my calendar (Fantastical) and my to do list (Things). 

![Fantastical and Things][image-11]{: width="350" }

Touch ID is definitely slower than Face ID, but I wear an Apple Watch each day and unlocking with the watch is fast. In some ways I prefer Touch ID. I wear glasses, but sometimes I like to use my phone when my glasses are off. With my previous iPhone with Face ID, I would have to put my glasses back on whenever I needed to enter a password or unlock the phone.

## Conclusion

I am loving the iPhone Duo lifestyle, so far. It is fantastic to have an iPhone mini in my pocket and an iPad mini with me at all times. I love reading on it with the Kindle app, using it to browse photos, read articles, take notes with Apple Pencil, and watch video. This will be an interested year as more apps get updated for the iPhone Duo. I will revisit this post when iPhone Duo 2 comes out.
